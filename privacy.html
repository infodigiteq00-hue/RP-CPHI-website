Privacy policy

Expo Meetings · last updated 30 October 2026

Expo Meetings is a meeting-booking and follow-up service for companies exhibiting at trade fairs. Each company has its own booking page and dashboard. This page explains what information the service keeps and why.

Visitors who book a meeting

When you book a meeting we keep what you enter in the form (such as your name, company, email, phone number, country and the products you're interested in) and the time you chose. It's stored in the exhibiting company's own Google Sheet and used only to arrange and follow up on your meeting. We send you emails about your booking. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else.

People a company invites

A company can upload a list of its contacts (first name, last name, email address and company name) to prepare invitations. The list is stored in that company's own Google Sheet, along with who has been marked as sent and when. Each mail is sent by a person at the company, from their own mailbox. To stop receiving these mails, reply and ask the company to take you off its list.

Companies that connect their Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. It saves the company pasting each mail ("Connect with Google"). For this the service asks Google for:

  • Manage drafts and send emails (gmail.compose): used only to create a draft of each mail the company prepares, which a person at the company then opens and sends from Gmail. The service never sends mail by itself and doesn't read, search or change the other email in the account.
  • Your email address and name (openid, email, profile): used to show which account is connected and as the sender's address.

Google's permission is kept on the service's server (Google Apps Script) and is never shown in the website or stored in the company's sheet.

The company can stop this at any time with "Disconnect" in its dashboard, which also withdraws the permission at Google, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Expo Meetings' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Keeping and deleting information

Information is kept for as long as the exhibiting company needs it for its trade fair meetings. To see, correct or delete your information, contact the company you met, or write to us.
