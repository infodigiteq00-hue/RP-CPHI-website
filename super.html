Leads

How we handle a lead
  1. Hot (interest 8 or more, or their expo is within 3 weeks): call or WhatsApp within 2 hours and offer a demo today or tomorrow. Invites need about 2 weeks to work.
  2. Warm (interest 5 to 7): email within 24 hours with two demo times. Cool: send the demo link and a 2-minute walkthrough, check back in 5 days.
  3. Before the demo: add them as a draft company from their website, so the demo shows their own branded booking page.
  4. The demo (15 minutes): their expo, their booth, their client list. End with a date for their invites to go out.
  5. Within 24 hours after: pricing, setup steps and a pilot for their next expo.
  6. No reply: follow up on day 2 and day 7, then 6 weeks before their next expo.

Next, once there's enough data: an instant reply with a recorded demo and a booking link, a short summary of what each lead asked before the call, and later a self-serve sign-up for leads who don't need a demo.

Outreach

How the outreach runs
  1. Expos: each Monday, expos 14 to 100 days away (with checked dates) are picked up. Too late is under 2 weeks: invites need time to work.
  2. Exhibitor list: fetched from the expo's public list page when its robots.txt allows it, otherwise pasted into the import form (from the organiser, a bought list or a copy of their directory).
  3. People, weekdays 06:00: Apollo finds 2 to 3 people per exhibitor (marketing first, then business development or export, then the MD), verified work emails only.
  4. Personal lines, 07:00: Claude reads each exhibitor's website and writes the opening line and a LinkedIn note. Nothing is invented.
  5. Emails, every 20 minutes in their working hours: email 1, a follow-up on day 3 and a last note on day 7, in one thread. Never more than the daily cap, never two first emails to one company on the same day, never to anyone who said no or bounced.
  6. Replies, every 5 minutes: interested replies stop the sequence for the whole company and email you. No, unsubscribe and bounces go on the do-not-contact list.
  7. LinkedIn, 08:30: up to 20 profiles a day with ready notes, in your morning email. German and Austrian exhibitors get LinkedIn only.
  8. After the show: the week after an expo, the same exhibitors get the follow-up pitch (their leads are going cold).
