    Questions exhibitors ask.

    Which expos does it work for?

    Any. Each show gets its own dates, hours, meeting length, booth and form questions, so one account covers all of them.

    Do my clients need an account?

    No. They open the link in their invite, pick a slot and give their details. They receive a calendar invite.

    What if two clients want the same slot?

    The first request wins. The second person is asked to choose another time, so you are never double booked.

    Does it work at the expo with bad internet?

    Open your dashboard once while online. After that, meetings, notes and voice notes work offline and upload when you reconnect. The meeting summary is written once you are back online.

    Does the AI send emails by itself?

    Never. It writes drafts. You read each one, edit it and send it from your own mailbox.

    Can the meeting summary get facts wrong?

    They only use what you said in your note. They do not add prices, dates or promises, and you can check and edit before anything is sent.

    Who can see my client list?

    Only your company's team, under your own login. Every company's data is stored separately.
